Rules for every phase

Extend proven foundations. Do not create competing histories or opaque agents.

Quality before volume

Prefer distinct worthwhile applications over maximum listing count.

Evidence before confidence

Unknown data stays neutral; every recommendation explains its evidence.

One durable history

Deepen the discovery ledger, consent state, and workspace—do not duplicate them.

User action is explicit

Opening a page is not success. “Done” triggers verification before continuation.

Source-generic by default

Improve typed browser primitives and adapters, not board-specific product policy.

Release claims need proof

Unit coverage, visual evidence, live disposable runs, and safety assertions travel together.

Roadmap

Phases describe dependency order. QoL and accessibility can ship continuously when they do not distract from trust, recovery, or state integrity.

0

Correct trust defects and measure reality

Fix known ranking errors first, then create one performance and quality baseline before optimizing larger flows.

Immediate · first delivery
CriticalTrustSmall

Correct salary and Easy Apply scoring

Missing salary must remain unknown and neutral. Easy Apply belongs to application effort, never candidate suitability.

Accept when: unknown pay adds zero; convenience cannot raise fit; existing corpus remains explainable.

CriticalTrustMedium

Separate ranking dimensions

Show suitability, preference alignment, compensation confidence, application effort, and evidence confidence instead of one overloaded number.

Accept when: users can explain every dimension from cited job/profile evidence.

HighDiscoveryMedium

Compute each assessment once per run

Build one candidate evidence index and carry each job assessment through budgeting, merging, and persistence.

Accept when: assessment equality is preserved and duplicate CPU work disappears in profiling.

CriticalPlatformMedium

Create a performance evidence module

Record time to first useful result, stage latency, p50/p95, fallbacks, workspace refresh, repeat-run cost, and provider/adapter method.

Accept when: discovery, source-debug, résumé import, generation, and apply reports include comparable latency data.

NextPlatformSmall

Start performance budgets as warnings

Gather representative local/live baselines first; promote stable budgets to release gates only after variance is understood.

Accept when: regressions are visible without creating flaky CI from network variability.

1

Unified “Needs you” action handoff

Turn sign-in, signup, verification, MFA, CAPTCHA, account-choice, manual answers, uploads, and redirects into one persisted and verifiable workflow.

Critical product feature
CriticalApplyMedium

Persist a typed UserActionRequest

Unify derived source prompts and application consent records with scoped kinds, states, revisions, exact URLs, checkpoint references, and event history.

Accept when: every request survives restart and stale/double clicks are idempotent.

CriticalApplyMedium

Add a global Action Inbox

Group pending actions by source/job with Open page, I’m done—verify, Skip, and Cancel controls plus concise local/external data labels.

Accept when: all pending human actions are findable, keyboard reachable, and deep-link to their context.

CriticalApplyLarge

Verify before resuming

“Done” moves to checking—not approved. Verify authentication, expected origin, challenge absence, field state, or manual answer without reading secrets.

Accept when: unresolved blockers remain truthful and repeated identical failures stop looping.

CriticalApplyLarge

Resume only the blocked work

Reuse the live page when safe or replay idempotent preparation from a checkpoint. Park one blocked queue job while independent jobs continue.

Accept when: prior results and prepared fields survive; final/ambiguous controls are never replayed.

NextApplyMedium

Preserve specific action kinds

Login, signup, email verification, MFA, CAPTCHA, account choice, legal consent, manual answer, upload, and redirect retain distinct copy and policy.

Accept when: no specific blocker degrades into a generic “manual verification” card.

2

History-aware, freshness-aware discovery

Use the existing discovery ledger before expensive work, while still detecting changed, reactivated, or stale listings.

Highest performance return
CriticalDiscoveryMedium

Build a target-scoped KnownPostingIndex

Give adapters compact stable identities, disposition, detail quality, timestamps, and fingerprints before detail pages or model work.

Accept when: unchanged known jobs invoke zero detail, vision, or LLM work.

HighDiscoveryMedium

Strengthen identity and URL aliases

Normalize tracking parameters and trailing slashes; prefer provider/source IDs; retain aliases without collapsing unrelated cross-source jobs by title alone.

Accept when: URL variants map once while same-title different jobs remain distinct.

HighDiscoveryMedium

Add content fingerprints and freshness

Track provider-updated time, card/detail hashes, last verified time, and a bounded TTL so changed jobs refresh and unchanged jobs stay cheap.

Accept when: same-ID changed listings re-enrich and card-only/inactive jobs revisit correctly.

CriticalDiscoverySmall

Budget by distinct newly retained roles

Raw validated rows, duplicates, and previously known jobs remain telemetry but cannot reduce the useful budget for later sources.

Accept when: duplicate-heavy early sources cannot starve new roles from later sources.

HighDiscoveryMedium

Stream partial results safely

Persist and display useful target results while slow sources continue, retaining complete results through cancellation or failure.

Accept when: time to first useful job improves and interruption preserves completed targets.

QoLDiscoverySmall

Show “What changed?” and source health

Summarize new, updated, reactivated, inactive, known, and intentionally skipped jobs; show typical duration, last warning, yield, and retry-failed-source.

Accept when: repeat searches explain both useful changes and time saved.

3

Ranking users can trust and steer

Calibrate fit with a representative corpus and let explicit, reversible feedback improve ordering without silent preference changes.

Quality lane
HighTrustMedium

Explain each ranking dimension

Keep exact listing quotes, profile evidence, missing/unknown/conflict states, and scorer version visible from every recommendation.

Accept when: no user must reverse-engineer a magic score.

QoLTrustSmall

Add “Not interested because…”

Optional reason chips cover role, seniority, location, work mode, compensation, company, missing requirement, duplicate, and other.

Accept when: learning is visible, conservative, resettable, and never changes hard facts.

HighTrustMedium

Build a labeled fit calibration corpus

Cover senior engineering, nontechnical, career change, incomplete listings, misleading titles, regional eligibility, and hard requirement conflicts.

Accept when: ranking changes report precision/recall and counterexample regressions.

LaterTrustSmall

Explain why a rank changed

Compare scorer version, profile/preference revision, listing fingerprint, and user feedback between assessments.

Accept when: changed ordering is auditable instead of surprising.

4

Résumé extraction and generation quality

Make timeline repair, claim grounding, work-history coverage, and revision control first-class rather than expecting users to inspect a polished PDF blindly.

High-trust investment
HighRésuméMedium

Ship a focused “Fix timeline” flow

Resolve overlaps, gaps, ambiguous dates, duplicate/split records, and missing employer/title with batch confirmation before canonical writes.

Accept when: real benchmark date/work-history diagnostics become actionable and reversible.

CriticalRésuméLarge

Ground every generated claim

Classify each bullet/summary claim as supported, supported paraphrase, needs review, or unsupported and link exact canonical evidence.

Accept when: unsupported achievements and numeric claims never reach approval as fact.

HighRésuméMedium

Deepen original-versus-tailored comparison

Show added/removed claims, omitted or compacted jobs, reordered experience, keyword changes, duplicated language, and page-count effects.

Accept when: no work history silently disappears and every omission is reversible.

QoLRésuméMedium

Add draft history, undo, and rollback

Every AI or manual revision gets a clear saved state and can be restored without touching the immutable original CV.

Accept when: navigation, restart, and failed generation cannot lose the last good draft.

LaterRésuméMedium

Cache unchanged résumé analysis safely

Key parser/text/vision artifacts by source fingerprint, provider/version, and policy while preserving the parallel import decision for new files.

Accept when: unchanged re-imports avoid cost without reusing incompatible or stale analysis.

HighRésuméMedium

Expand the real-output quality gates

Measure professional tone, entailment, repetition, fragment quality, unsupported numbers, work-history coverage, rendering, and latency.

Accept when: weak “plausible sounding” prose is a benchmark failure, not subjective feedback.

5

Application readiness, memory, and recovery

Make preparation predictable before opening an ATS and let useful, user-approved answers compound across applications.

Workflow completion
HighApplySmall

Add a pre-apply readiness card

Name the exact CV/file, destination, sign-in/account likelihood, missing answers, expected blockers, possible intermediate writes, and final-submit boundary.

Accept when: the user knows what the run will and will not do before the browser opens.

HighApplyMedium

Save reviewed answers for future applications

“Use for this application” stays separate from “Save for future”; show the prior question, answer, confidence, and provenance before reuse.

Accept when: generic memory never silently overwrites a job-specific answer.

HighApplyMedium

Make queues independently recoverable

Retry one, retry failed, park blocked, skip, cancel, and restage remaining work while preserving completed preparation and quiet summaries.

Accept when: one signup/login gate cannot freeze unrelated jobs and restart loses nothing.

QoLApplySmall

Export a local application packet

Bundle listing, selected CV identity, prepared answers, evidence, destination, and checkpoint state for manual completion or support.

Accept when: export contains no credentials, raw private browser state, or false submitted status.

6

Workspace scale, resilience, and paid-product polish

Keep actions fast and state trustworthy as users accumulate years of jobs, résumés, applications, runs, and evidence.

Parallel hardening lane
ArchitecturePlatformLarge

Replace full snapshots with typed deltas

Use slice projections and atomic repository upserts instead of reading, parsing, rewriting, and returning the entire workspace after small changes.

Accept when: large-workspace action latency stays stable and crash checkpoint semantics remain intact.

HighPlatformMedium

Benchmark realistic long-lived workspaces

Exercise 10k ledger entries, 1k saved jobs, years of applications, migrations, restart recovery, indexed lookups, compaction, and retention.

Accept when: lookup is indexed, mutations remain responsive, and old evidence has deliberate retention controls.

QoLPlatformSmall

Show saved state and offer undo

Profile, Settings, résumés, and answers display saving/saved/failed states; meaningful edits get versioned undo and recovery.

Accept when: users cannot accidentally navigate away from an uncertain save state.

QoLPlatformMedium

Add a background task strip

Show current stage, source, live counters, pause/cancel/resume, and history-based remaining-time ranges—never a fake countdown.

Accept when: partial, paused, stale, cancelled, failed, and complete states always expose the next action.

TrustPlatformSmall

Add local/external data receipts

Label what stayed local, what was sent to a configured model, what file was attached externally, and which intermediate site writes occurred.

Accept when: every application can produce a truthful, redacted action and privacy receipt.

QualityPlatformMedium

Formal accessibility and responsive pass

Verify keyboard order, focus restoration, live status, screen-reader labels, contrast, overflow, 1024×720, narrow panes, zoom, and high DPI.

Accept when: primary flows are fully reachable without pointer input and never rely on color alone.

Explicitly not building

These exclusions keep the roadmap aligned with current product goals and safety decisions.

  • Automatic final-submit clicking.
  • Credential, password, OTP, CAPTCHA, or email-token storage.
  • Automated account creation or security-challenge bypass.
  • Inferred login success just because the browser opened.
  • Source-specific routing hacks in shared discovery.
  • A second discovery history, consent store, or scoring engine.
  • Opaque learning that silently changes ranking.
  • Mandatory tailoring or replacement of original-CV mode.
  • Silent résumé edits, invented claims, or hidden work-history removal.
  • Sending the full historical ledger to a language model.
  • Mandatory cloud accounts, sync, or surveillance analytics before local reliability.
  • Hidden Interview Helper or unauthorized capture-evasion behavior.
  • A browser substrate rewrite without a concrete measured capability gap.
  • Maximizing listing volume at the expense of distinct worthwhile roles.
